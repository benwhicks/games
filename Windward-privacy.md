# Windward Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

Windward is a sailing and trade game for iOS. It does not collect, store or share any personal information on servers of its own, because it has none.

## What the app stores on your device

Your settings, high scores, achievements, a puzzle saved with "Save and exit", and the date of your last free game are kept on your device only. They are never sent to the developer or to any third party. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Game Center (Apple)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app uses Apple's Game Center for online games, achievements and leaderboards. This is handled by Apple, not by the developer, and is covered by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

- **Leaderboards:** Your progress in the trade part of the game is sent to the Game Center if you are signed in, and your Game Center name appears on the leaderboard, along with the names of anyone you played that game with.
- **Achievements** you earn may be reported to Game Center.

If you don't want this, sign out of Game Center in your device's Settings. The game works without it.

## Purchases

The optional unlock is an in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple, along with any expansion packs. The developer does not see your payment details or Apple Account.

## What the app does not do

It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party code that collects data. It does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. 

## Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
