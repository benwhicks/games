Contranymium is a word game where the score comes from what your words mean, not from rare letters. Every word you play is compared with the words already on the board. The more closely it fits the board’s growing theme, the more it’s worth. 

Hunt for *contranyms*, the words that mean two opposite things, for big bonuses. Words that mean different things, *homonyms*, also get a bonus, as does finding *homophones* (same sound, different spelling) of words already played. Play solo, share a device with a friend, or play cooperatively online, where up to four players pool one board, one score and one limited set of moves.

Simple to learn, and every word you play is a small argument about what belongs together.
