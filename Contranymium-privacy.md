# Contranymium Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

Contranymium is a word game for iPhone. It does not collect, store or share any personal information on servers of its own, because it has none.

## What the app stores on your device

Your settings, high scores, achievements, a puzzle saved with "Save and exit", and the date of your last free game are kept on your device only. They are never sent to the developer or to any third party. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Game Center (Apple)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app uses Apple's Game Center for online games, achievements and leaderboards. This is handled by Apple, not by the developer, and is covered by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

- **Online games** are played directly between the players' devices through Game Center. Your Game Center name is shown to the other players in the game.
- **Leaderboards:** when you finish a game, your score is sent to Game Center, and your Game Center name appears on the leaderboard, along with the names of anyone you played that game with.
- **Achievements** you earn may be reported to Game Center.

If you don't want this, sign out of Game Center in your device's Settings. The rest of the game works without it.

## Purchases

The optional unlock is an in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. The developer does not see your payment details or Apple Account.

## What the app does not do

It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party code that collects data. It does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

## Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
