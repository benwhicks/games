

Sail back to Nusuntara, when the spice really did flow. Leverage real sailing physics to make the wind your ally, keep an eye to windward lest the weather turns on you.

Where the spice grows many come seeking fortune. Getting it home is another matter.

Windward is a sailing game set in the monsoon seas of about 1300 AD, where cloves, nutmeg, mace and pepper were worth more than their weight in silver, and where the wind decided who got rich and who went down with the cargo.

The weather is your ally, the weather is your enemy.
Sail the way sailors did. Read the wind and trim your sail to it. Catch the monsoon on your quarter and watch the knots build, or catch fish on the reef. Use the tide to slip out of a harbour. Let a following swell lift you toward home. The sea is built on real physics, and the weather on modelled pressure and thermodynamics, so good seamanship is rewarded. The same sea that carries you can turn on you. A squall line builds on the horizon and a gale front brings the sky down. Reefs break the swell into surf, the stream sets you toward the rocks, and a mast overloaded in a gust goes over the side. 

You begin in Nusuntara, the "outer islands", in the time of the Majapahit empire. In 1300AD their marintime dominance was built on master shipwrights using the native teak to Java, and leveraging the changing monsoons to travel, explore and trade. Play for riches and new ships, or simply sail and discover an ocean world lost to time.
